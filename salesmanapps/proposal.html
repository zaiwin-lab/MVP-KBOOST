Retail Sales & Distribution Management App

A complete field-sales system for salesmen who carry stock — customer, outlet, order, delivery, stock, payment, return, ranking and referral, in one app.

16 Modules3 Transaction ModelsSalesman Mobile FirstThermal 58mm / 80mm

What this system does

A salesman opens the app in the morning, sees his day, drives to each shop, and handles everything at the counter: take the order, deliver, invoice, collect payment, print the receipt on a thermal printer and share it on WhatsApp — then watches his ranking move in real time.

Behind him, every transaction is filed against a Customer Code that belongs to the company. Every document carries its own running number. Nothing lives in a salesman's private notebook or phone.

The core concept

Customer Profile = Command Center

The salesman does not hunt through menus. He opens the customer, and every action and every record for that outlet is on one screen.

Three transaction models

Payment Term is not just a due date. Each type runs a genuinely different transaction flow, and the system controls stock and billing differently for each.
📦 Consignment

Bill only what was sold

Stock is delivered to the outlet first. The customer is not charged for everything delivered — only for what actually sold. On the next visit the salesman counts the balance on the shelf, and the system works out the rest.

📝 Bil to Bil

Every order is a bill

Each order becomes its own bill, paid according to the agreed term of 1, 2 or 3 months. Next month's order becomes a new, separate bill.

The 16 modules

01Register Customer / OutletUnique Customer Code (1 kod = 1 customer), full details, SSM, outlet photo, GPS latitude & longitude saved permanently, and the Payment Term that governs the account.
02Senarai OutletAll registered outlets with search by Customer Code, outlet name, PIC, phone or WhatsApp. Shows outstanding, payment term and account status at a glance.
03Jadual HarianThe list of shops to visit today, in travel order, with remarks and shortcuts to Navigate, WhatsApp and Profile. Not attendance, not visit tracking — no pending/completed status.
04Order + Harga TweakProduct, quantity, price, discount and remark. Where permission allows, price can be tweaked — and the system records the original price, the tweaked price, who changed it, and when.
05DO — Delivery OrderRaised from the Order, with its own running number, delivery address and delivery details.
06SO — Sales OrderRaised from the DO, carrying pricing, discount, total and payment term forward.
07InvoiceRaised from the SO, with SST where required, due date derived from the payment term, and live payment status.
08PaymentRecorded from the Customer Profile or straight from the Invoice. Full or partial. Updates outstanding, history and payment-term control.
09Payment ReceiptEvery payment produces a receipt with its own running number, amount, method, outstanding balance and staff name.
10Product & StockProduct list with SKU, category, image, unit, price and minimum stock. Stock in/out, adjustment, balance, return stock and consignment stock, all tracked.
11ReturnA separate transaction that does not pass through DO/SO/Invoice. Reason, photo and remark captured; updates stock and customer history.
12Sales RankingAll salesmen ranked by total sales, recalculated in real time on every sale. Today, this week, this month, this year, custom or all time.
13PDF · WhatsApp · Direct PrintEvery document exports to PDF, shares to the customer on WhatsApp, and prints direct to a 58mm or 80mm thermal printer. No A4, no A5.
14Running NumberSix independent auto-generated sequences, never duplicated, shown on screen, on the PDF, on WhatsApp and on the printed slip.
15Payment & Stock ControlThe engine behind the three models — controls how stock moves and how a bill is produced for Consignment, Bil to Bil and Cash.
16Refer & EarnUnique referral code and link per user, referral tracking, and 5% commission on every qualifying referral, with paid and pending commission history.
