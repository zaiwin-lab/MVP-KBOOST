# The 60-Second Pitch Path

The exact click sequence for Coach Aril. Rehearse once; it runs in about 60 seconds
and tells the whole story without a single slide.

Open `index.html`. It always starts on the **Manager Dashboard**, 19 Aug 2026, with
the same numbers every time — nothing is random, so nothing surprises you on stage.

---

### 0:00 — Open cold on the Dashboard
**Say:** *"This is Monday morning. You have not called anyone yet."*

Let the numbers count up on their own. Point at the four tiles: 6 salesmen active,
9 of 18 outlets covered, today's sales, collection rate.

### 0:08 — The AI briefing
**Point at the periwinkle panel.** Read one line out loud:

> *"RM 50,841 is sitting overdue across 16 invoices — that is the number to chase before month end."*

**Say:** *"Nobody typed that. It read every row so you don't have to."*

### 0:15 — Switch to Salesman
Top right, click **Salesman**. The phone appears.

**Say:** *"Same system. This is Ahmad, standing in a shop in Bangsar."*

### 0:20 — The AI beat plan
**Point at "AI suggested next stop".** Elegance Scents Bangsar is first, and the
chips say why: **RM 13,124 overdue · High value avg RM 6,396 · Tier A**.

**Say:** *"It did not sort alphabetically. It sorted by who owes you money."*

### 0:26 — Check in
Tap the first outlet → **Check in at this outlet**. The status flips to Visited with
a GPS tag and a timestamp.

**Say:** *"You now know he is actually there. Not 'on the way'."*

### 0:33 — Take an order, and get caught
Tap **Take order**. Tap `+` a few times on **Oud Royale 50ml**.
Now change the unit price to **130** and pause.

The gold AI panel appears: *below the RM 155.00 floor price.*

**Say:** *"This is the one that pays for the system. He cannot quietly discount your
margin away. It catches it before it is submitted."*

Set the price back to **189**, tap **Confirm order**.

### 0:42 — Invoice + coaching, instantly
The invoice is already made — real number, terms pulled from that outlet's 30-day
credit, correct due date. Above it, the AI coaching nudge.

**Say:** *"No paperwork tonight. And it just coached him on what to sell next visit."*

### 0:48 — Collect and receipt
**Record payment** → leave the amount → **Confirm payment**.
Receipt appears stamped. Tap **Send via WhatsApp** to show the customer message.

**Say:** *"Customer has the receipt before he leaves the shop."*

### 0:54 — The ranking moves
Tap **See my ranking →**. The leaderboard has already changed.

**Say:** *"Every salesman sees this. It runs itself."*

### 0:58 — The closing argument
Switch back to **Manager** → **Client Data** → click
**Simulate: Ahmad Razif resigns**.

**Say:** *"He quits tomorrow and takes his phone with him. Watch."*

The banner turns green: **zero records lost**, outlets reassigned automatically.

> **This is the close.** Every outlet, order and payment belonged to the company the
> whole time, not to the man who walked out. Ask: *"What happens today when your best
> salesman leaves?"* — then stop talking.

Click **Reset demo** to run it again for the next prospect.

## If they ask "what else does it do?"
Go to **Full Build** in the sidebar. Six modules working, ten scoped and named.
That page is the upsell — it shows you have thought past the demo.

## Recovery
Anything goes sideways: reload the page. State resets to the exact opening position.
There is no server and no login, so nothing can be in a bad state.
